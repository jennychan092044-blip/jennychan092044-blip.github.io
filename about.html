Our story

An original patent factory, not a trading company.

BeeHappy Technology was founded in 2020 in Shenzhen — China's hardware capital. We design, tool and manufacture every product in our catalog. No middlemen, no rebranded generics.

Who we are

We make small gadgets that solve big outdoor problems.

BeeHappy started with a single product — a portable camp shower that could be hung on a tree branch and run for 40 minutes on a detachable battery pack. It became a quiet hit on Amazon and a viral favorite among overlanders, RVers, and weekend campers.

From that first product, we expanded into smart outdoor showers, foldable bidet sprayers for Muslim-friendly travel, mini portable bidets for postpartum and elderly care, and a 3-in-1 portable air pump that took the place of three separate devices. Then came USB handheld mini fans for hot-summer commutes, a one-touch electric water pump for bottled water, a 5-speed foldable cooling fan, and a mason jar electric vacuum sealer for kitchen freshness.

Eleven original products today. Twelve patents and industrial designs. Two hundred B2B partners across fifty countries. One factory. One team. No shortcuts.

Why choose us

Three things you won't find at a typical trading company.

Original patents. The camp shower head, the detachable battery pack, the foldable bidet tank, the air-pump nozzle set — these are our own designs, not licensed copies. Your brand will not be sued for IP infringement, and you can market the originality of your product.

Lower MOQ on real OEM. We can produce 500-piece OEM color runs on most product lines. That's a real number for a real brand launch, not a 5,000-piece minimum that locks small buyers out.

Communication that works. Direct WhatsApp line to your account manager. Spec sheet, sample photos and production photos shared as work progresses. No middlemen mis-translating your emails.
