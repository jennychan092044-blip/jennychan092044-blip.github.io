FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short, direct answers to the questions our customers ask most often.

What does BeeHappy Technology actually make?

BeeHappy Technology is an original patent designer and OEM/ODM manufacturer. We make 11 original products: portable camp showers, USB rechargeable outdoor showers, smart outdoor showers with temperature display, foldable electric bidet sprayers, mini portable bidets, portable air pumps, USB handheld mini fans, electric water pumps, foldable cooling fans and mason jar vacuum sealers. Everything is designed, tooled and quality-controlled in-house in Shenzhen, China.

Do you offer OEM / ODM / private label?

Yes — OEM, ODM and private label are our core business. We support color customization, logo printing on housing, gift-box design, retail packaging, retail-ready product inserts, Amazon FBA labeling, and custom product specifications. Minimum order quantity varies by product; selected items accept low MOQ for new brands.

What certifications do your products carry?

Our products are certified to CE, RoHS, FCC where applicable, and our showers carry IPX7 or IPX8 waterproof ratings. Battery products use LFP cells for safer operation and longer cycle life. Specific certificates and MSDS / UN38.3 documents are provided per shipment.

What is the lead time and shipping method?

Sample lead time: 3-7 days for in-stock designs, 15-25 days for custom OEM. Bulk production: 25-40 days after sample approval. We ship by sea (FCL/LCL), air freight, or express (DHL / FedEx / UPS) depending on urgency and destination. We have shipped to 50+ countries.
